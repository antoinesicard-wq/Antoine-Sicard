Academic Profile & Research Achievements

Antoine Sicard completed his MD in nephrology and transplantation at Paris V University in 2013, following a highly ranked national medical residency examination in France (123rd among 6,500 candidates). During his residency, he completed a fellowship at the Cleveland Clinic (Ohio, USA). He subsequently obtained a PhD in transplant immunology from Lyon 1 University in 2016 and completed a postdoctoral fellowship in Prof. Megan Levings’ laboratory at the University of British Columbia (Vancouver, Canada), focusing on CAR Treg cell therapy.

He currently holds a Professor of Medicine position at Nice University Hospital (PU-PH, Nice, France), where he serves as Head of the Department of Nephrology, Dialysis and Transplantation. He also leads the TNT research team within the Laboratory of PhysioMolecular Medicine (UMR 7370, CNRS, Université Côte d’Azur), where he serves as Co-Director of the institute. His research focuses on the translational development of immune evasion platforms for autologous and allogeneic cell therapies, as well as transplant desensitization and resistance to ischemia–reperfusion injury.

He has authored more than 60 peer-reviewed publications in leading journals, secured over €2 million in competitive academic research funding, and is the co-founder of Therallo (founded in 2025), a company developing scalable immune evasion platforms for cell-based therapies.
